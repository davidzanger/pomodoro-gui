# Pomodoro timer

This context describes the work and break phases of a Pomodoro cycle.

## Language

**Pomodoro**:
A regular work phase that starts a cycle and counts toward the next long break.

**Additional Pomodoro**:
A short work phase after a Pomodoro. It can repeat while the user postpones the break; repetitions do not count as new Pomodoros.

**Break**:
The short or long rest phase due after a Pomodoro and its Additional Pomodoros.

**Break decision**:
The choice to keep working through repeating Additional Pomodoros or begin the due break. It can remain open while Additional Pomodoros continue; dismissing it keeps the current phase running.
